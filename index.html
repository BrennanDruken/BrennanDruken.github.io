Space Catalog

This website contains a catalog of minor planets, comets, moons and exomoons, planets and exoplanets, stars, galaxies, and more.

Clicking on the "Catalog" button brings you to a page containing different types of celestial objects. Clicking on a type of object will bring you to a list of objects of that type, either sorted alphabetically, numerically, or both. If you click on an object, it will bring you to a page that contains information about it.
